Privacy Policy for AspirantX: Focus Galaxy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Overview & Commitment

AspirantX ("Focus Galaxy", "we", "us", or "our") is built with a privacy-first foundation for students, civil services candidates, and competitive exam aspirants. We respect your personal privacy and strictly minimize the data collected. We do not sell your personal data or track your browsing outside the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process only the minimal data necessary to provide personalized study progression and account sync:

  • Account Credentials: If you sign in, we process your email address and authentication tokens via our secure authentication provider (Supabase) to persist your study progress across devices.
  • Focus & Study Telemetry: Study session duration, chosen subjects/topics, streak counts, and earned Cosmic Dust. This information is saved locally in your device storage and backed up securely to your user profile.
  • Device & Hardware Optimization State: Battery level indicators (read locally in browser via Battery API) to automatically activate battery-saving mode (throttling 3D graphics from 60 FPS to 30 FPS). This information is never transmitted or sold.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

The Android version of AspirantX uses the following native permissions exclusively for user-beneficial features:

  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps your screen active while a Pomodoro focus timer is running so your phone display does not sleep while studying. This lock is immediately released on pause or completion.
  • VIBRATE: Provides gentle haptic feedback on button taps and celebration vibration pulses when study milestones are claimed.
  • INTERNET: Enables encrypted HTTPS synchronization of study progress, PYQs, and syllabus items with your secure account.
  • LIVE_WALLPAPER & NOTIFICATIONS (Optional): Allows users to optionally set dynamic daily revision wallpapers or receive study schedule alerts.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising & No Data Selling

AspirantX does NOT sell, rent, or trade student data, study habits, or contact information to data brokers, third-party advertisers, or marketing firms. All audio soundscapes and sound effects are synthesized locally on your device using code-only Web Audio API waveforms without loading external tracking scripts or remote media assets.

5. Security & Encryption in Transit

All communications between your device and our servers utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Sensitive database rows and authentication keys are isolated using PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).

6. Data Deletion & Account Control

You retain full ownership of your data. You may request complete deletion of your account and associated study records at any time by navigating to your Profile Settings inside the app or by emailing our data protection officer directly.
